# Anti-Codex Findings

## 2026-06-20 Rounded Button Hit Testing
- Chromium/Electron hit testing can exclude the extreme rounded corners of a button even though the visual control appears to occupy the full bounding rectangle. A center-only test cannot detect this.
- For controls that must accept the full rectangular touch target, keep the interactive element rectangular and draw the rounded appearance on a pointer-events-disabled child or pseudo-element. Verify a grid of interior points, not only the center.
